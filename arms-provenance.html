Everything in the app

One place for the details, documents and history of everything in your safe.

Record everything you own

  • Firearms: pistols, revolvers, rifles, shotguns, SBRs, SBSs, machine guns, muzzleloaders
  • Suppressors and bows & crossbows as their own categories (Full)
  • Accessories such as optics, magazines, lights, holsters and safes, with quantity, linked to the gun they live on
  • Acquisition details: who from, how, when, price, transferring FFL, importer, extra serials
  • Condition on a standard scale, with NRA Antique grades applied automatically for pre-1899 pieces
  • Photos and documents: set a cover photo, tag serial-number photos, attach receipts and PDFs

Ammunition & archery

  • Box & label scanner: reads the barcode or printed label on an ammo box, on your phone
  • Remembers barcodes, so the next box of the same load takes one scan
  • Scanned boxes add to matching stock, with Undo
  • Shotgun shell details, plus arrows, bolts and broadheads
  • Safe count changes with a running history of use and restocks
  • Low-stock alerts, favorites and duplicate-lot checks

NFA, trusts & estate

  • ATF Control # and optional paper tax stamp serial on NFA items
  • Gun trusts: trust name, date, trustees and the trust document, with a Trust badge and filter
  • Heir planning: heir and alternate heir per item, executor, and where keys and paperwork are kept
  • Estate Report grouped by heir, with signature lines. It's an attachment to a will, not a will.

Heir planning and the Estate Report are part of Full.

Paperwork in a tap (PDF)

  • Item Sheets for one item or many in one PDF, with photos and receipts, for insurance or police reports
  • Bill of Sale for one item or several, with an itemized table and optional witness and notary blocks
  • Collection Report, Archive Report and Ammo Log
  • A&D Ledger as PDF or CSV, with optional C&R (FFL 03) columns
  • Serial masking: show only the last 4 characters on anything you share

PDF reports are part of Full. The current-inventory Ammo Log is free.

Organized your way

  • Tabs, search and filters by type, caliber, trust or storage location
  • Bulk actions: archive, delete, set type or mark cleaned for many items at once
  • Archive with a reason (sold, traded, gifted, lost, stolen, transferred to heir), with Undo and Restore
  • Cleaning log and reminders per item

Your data stays yours

Private by design, not by promise

Most apps ask you to trust their servers. Arms Provenance doesn't have any.

  • No internet permission. It's removed from the app at build time.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to breach.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Encrypted on your phone, and Android cloud backup is turned off for the app.
  • The camera is only for scanning ammo boxes. Each photo is read on your phone and then discarded.
  • Backups are encrypted with a password only you know.
  • You decide what leaves the phone. PDFs, CSVs and backups go only where you send them.
  • Serial masking on reports you share.

Questions

Does Arms Provenance need the internet?

No. The app doesn't request Android's internet permission, so it can't connect to anything. Links in Laws & Resources open in your phone's own browser.

Where is my data stored? Can you see it?

Only on your phone, in an encrypted database. There's no account and no server, so we have no copy and no way to see what you enter.

What happens if I lose or replace my phone?

Because nothing is in the cloud, keep an encrypted backup. The app makes a password-protected backup file and can remind you on a schedule. Store the file somewhere you trust, then restore it on your new phone.

What does the scanner read?

Ammunition boxes: the barcode, or the printed label if there's no barcode it knows. Everything is read on your phone, and the photo is discarded right after. You always review the details before anything is saved.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Use a 4 to 8 digit PIN, with optional fingerprint or face unlock. The app locks again when you leave it for more than a minute.

Will the free version stop working?

No. Lite is free for good. Limits only stop you adding more items. Nothing you've entered is ever hidden, locked or deleted, and archived items don't count toward the limit.

Is it a subscription?

No. Ammo and Full are planned as one-time unlocks. Pricing is still being finalized and may change before launch.

Is the A&D Ledger a compliance record?

Arms Provenance is a personal record-keeping tool, not a compliance tool. Private collectors are not federally required to keep an A&D bound book. Federal licensees, including Curio & Relic (FFL 03) collectors, are responsible for their own records under 27 CFR 478.125, which must be bound or kept in an ATF-approved alternate format. Check your state and local laws.

Can I buy or sell items through the app?

No. Arms Provenance is a private record book. The Bill of Sale is a document you print and sign for your own records. The app doesn't list, sell or transfer anything.

Is there an iPhone or desktop version?

Arms Provenance is Android only for now. Email us if you'd like to hear when other versions arrive.
