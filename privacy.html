Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Draft update: November 4, 2026 (pending review)

The short version: Arms Provenance does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no internet permission, stores your records encrypted on your device, and works without an account. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

This policy explains how Security Provenance Solutions LLC (“Security Provenance,” “we,” “us”) handles information for the website at securityprovenance.com (the “Website”) and the Arms Provenance Android application (the “App”).

1. Arms Provenance app

Information we collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or usage data with us or with anyone else. We have no servers that receive App data and no way to see what you enter.

No internet access

The App does not request Android’s internet permission. It cannot connect to the internet, so it cannot send your information anywhere. Links in the App, such as ATF and state law resources, open in your device’s own web browser.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (for example item details, serial numbers, photos, documents, and notes) is stored only on your device in an encrypted database. It is not synced to any cloud service by the App, and Android’s automatic cloud backup is turned off for the App. You can protect the App with a PIN and, optionally, your device’s fingerprint or face unlock. Biometric checks are handled by Android, and the App never receives your biometric data.

Camera

If you choose to use the ammunition box scanner, the App uses your device camera to read the barcode or printed label on an ammo box. This recognition is performed entirely on your device using Google ML Kit’s on-device text recognition and barcode scanning. The scan photo is processed locally, deleted right after it is read, and never uploaded or shared. You can also take or choose photos of your items, which are stored only in the App’s encrypted storage. You can deny or revoke camera permission at any time in Android settings; the rest of the App will continue to work.

No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking

The App has no sign-up or login, shows no advertising, and contains no analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not use advertising IDs.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders (for example cleaning due, low ammunition, or backup reminders), the App schedules notifications locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

In-app purchases

Optional unlocks are purchased through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy, and the App only receives a confirmation of which unlocks you own, which it keeps on your device. We do not receive your payment details. If we offer purchases on this Website in the future, a third-party payment provider will process them under its own privacy policy, and this policy will be updated before then.

Your control: export, backup, and delete

  • Backup: The App can create a password-protected, encrypted backup file that you save or share wherever you choose.
  • Export: You may export your records (CSV and PDF reports) at any time. Exported files are saved where you choose, and once they leave the App, you are responsible for where you store or share them.
  • Delete: You can delete individual records within the App. You can delete all App data by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

Because your data exists only on your device, we cannot recover it for you if it is deleted or if the device is lost. Keep your own backups using the App’s backup feature.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any data, because the App does not give us any.

2. This website

  • The Website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers, and it does not load fonts or scripts from third parties.
  • The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address in server logs to deliver and secure the service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details. We do not receive or use this information to identify you.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and do not share it or add you to a mailing list.

3. Children’s privacy

The App and Website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

4. Security

The App encrypts your records at rest and relies on Android’s app sandbox and device security. Protect your device with a screen lock to keep your data safe. If you believe you have found a security issue, please email info@securityprovenance.com.

5. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above. If a future version of the App ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
