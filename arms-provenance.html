Arms Provenance

A private, offline, encrypted log for firearms, archery, suppressors, ammunition, and gear. Your records stay on your device. Period.

AndroidComing soon to Google PlayNo internet permission

Features

Everything you need to keep accountability of your equipment, and nothing you don't.

Log everything in one place

Firearms, bows and crossbows, suppressors, ammunition, optics, and accessories, with the details you choose to track: make, model, serial, purchase info, and notes.

On-device scanning

Use your camera to read serial numbers and barcodes with on-device text and barcode recognition. Images are processed on your phone and never uploaded.

Encrypted at rest

Your records are stored in an encrypted database on your device, protected by Android security features.

Your data, your exports

Export your records when you decide to, for insurance, estate planning, or your own backups. Delete everything at any time.

Works fully offline

The app does not request internet permission. It cannot send your data anywhere, even if it wanted to.

No account, no ads

No sign-up, no login, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Privacy first, by design

A record of what you own is sensitive information. Arms Provenance was built so that the only person who ever sees it is you.

Read the full privacy policy

  • Offline only. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot transmit data.
  • Encrypted. Your records are encrypted and stored locally on your device.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for. No email, no password, no profile.
  • No ads or tracking. No analytics, no advertising IDs, no third-party trackers.
  • Camera stays local. Scanning happens on-device; images are never uploaded.
  • You are in control. Export or delete your data whenever you choose.
